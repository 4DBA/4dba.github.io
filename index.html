Don't Let Completeness Kidnap Your Thinking

发表于 2026-09-30 分类于 Life

I set out to write Tea of Four Seasons after a small revelation about jasmine tea. But the moment I started drafting, my mind slipped into an imposing “four seasons” frame: Longjing in spring, a bowl of liuyueshuang in summer, osmanthus oolong in autumn, black tea in winter. From there it seemed only natural to map the seasons onto a life—sweet drinks as a child, caffeine at work, flower teas with age, and finally a return to pure, plain tea.

Halfway through, I realized the frame was fake. I had no deep feelings about black tea. At twenty-five I happily drink soda, coffee, flower tea, and plain tea—all at once. If something tastes good, I’m in. I don’t write for a living. Why force a “complete” structure and write for the sake of writing?

Tea of Four Seasons

发表于 2026-09-29 更新于 2026-09-30 分类于 Life

In the height of July, a friend and I each hunted down two jasmine teas—one a famous brand everyone was talking about, the other a younger label popular with our generation—and set them side by side. Each had its charm. Brewing filled the room with an almost aggressive floral perfume, and the summer restlessness settled a little.

Then, near the end of September, something odd happened.

At my desk one afternoon, the bright jasmine scent had thinned—nothing like the sweetness of midsummer. The office stays climate-controlled; green tea and oolong hold up fine. Jasmine shouldn’t go dull that fast. I puzzled over it, even suspected a blend of stale leaves.

Then over the National Day holiday, with family at a hotel, I drank a casually steeped cup of osmanthus oolong. Chatting before dinner, with the first autumn wind, that warm osmanthus note lingered on the tongue—more healing than summer jasmine had ever been.

Only then did I understand:

It wasn’t the tea that had faded. My body had quietly changed with the season.

We never feel the seasons because the calendar turns a page. The tongue and the skin choose first.

我的老家是黄泽，爷爷奶奶住在黄泽的光明村。

从爷爷奶奶家出门，走几步路就能到黄泽江的堤坝旁。堤坝上方的江水蓄得满满的，流得一点儿也不急，平静得像一面镜子，倒映着蓝天和层层叠叠的矮山。山长得一点也不凶，山脊连绵，山头圆缓。一眼望去，山绵延得看不见尽头。可我一点都不觉得无力或害怕，因为我知道山的另一头就是热闹的嵊州城区，山从来都不是无尽的。这些山从不摆出一副高高在上的架势让人望而生畏，反而温和得像是在冲人招手：只要肯迈开脚，你就一定能走过去。每翻过一个小山头，脚下就多一份踏实，心里不仅不觉得累，反而更添了一股接着往前迈的劲头。

My Hometown, Huangze

发表于 2026-09-29 更新于 2026-09-30 分类于 Life

My hometown is Huangze. My grandparents live in Guangming Village there.

From their door it is only a few steps to the embankment of the Huangze River. Above the dike the water lies full and unhurried, a mirror holding blue sky and layers of low hills. The hills are not fierce: ridges unspool, summits stay round. They seem to run on without end—yet I never feel small or afraid, because I know the busy streets of Shengzhou wait on the other side. The mountains are not endless. They never stand aloof to overawe you; they are gentle, as if waving you on. Put one foot in front of the other and you will get there. Each little crest you cross leaves the ground firmer underfoot; instead of tiring, you gather another push to go on.

Deep in the folds of those hills run many nameless creeks—I cannot name them, but my grandparents can; they know every stream and every village. The creeks cut thin lines through the soil and slip quiet through bamboo and field ridges. Water does not speak; it simply flows. The Huangze River runs on and joins the Shan.

Oracle 23ai 新特性尝鲜：AI Vector Search 与 JSON Relational Duality

发表于 2026-06-03 更新于 2026-09-30 分类于 Oracle

本文是 Oracle 新特性系列的第 28 篇（收官篇），聚焦 Oracle 23ai 最核心的两个 AI 特性：AI Vector Search 与 JSON Relational Duality View。

一、问题背景

随着 LLM（大语言模型）和 RAG（Retrieval Augmented Generation）应用的爆发式增长，数据库不再只是存储结构化数据的仓库，它还需要具备向量检索和语义搜索的能力。传统做法是将向量数据存入专用的向量数据库（如 Milvus、Pinecone），但这意味着架构中多了一个组件，数据同步、一致性、运维复杂度都会随之增加。

Oracle 在 23ai 版本中正式将自己定位为 AI Database，核心思路是：让关系型数据库原生支持向量操作，无需引入外部向量库，就能在 SQL 层面完成向量存储、索引和相似度检索。同时，23ai 推出了 JSON Relational Duality View，彻底打通关系模型与文档模型之间的壁垒。

对于 DBA 而言，这些特性不是”锦上添花”，而是必须掌握的技能栈：

  • 应用团队越来越多地要求在数据库层做向量检索，而不是维护额外的向量库
  • JSON Duality View 让开发者用 JSON 接口操作关系表，DBA 需要理解其背后的机制才能做好性能调优
  • 23ai 的 AI 特性已经进入生产环境，早一步掌握就意味着早一步建立技术壁垒
