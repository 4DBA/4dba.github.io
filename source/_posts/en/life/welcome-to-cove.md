Hello, I'm Cove. Welcome to this small plot of ground that is mine.

If you look at the account ID—`Cove_Covers_IT`—you may already guess the joke. As a DBA who lives in the basement of data and systems, covering my back (Cover) is professional instinct.

Here, **IT** (uppercase) means hard infrastructure. I will share unreservedly: stubborn production incidents, database post-mortems, and the cold logic of low-level operations (*I can cover IT*).

**it** (lowercase) means the concrete stuff of daily life. When I try to share the load with AI assistants, or strike a balance between work and living, I want to record that fumbling and those small clearings—and reason things out with you (*I can cover it*).

So that you can find your way quickly in what follows, keep this little “how to read me” guide.

## Five fields

Content will orbit five areas:

- **DBA practice** — the core. Troubleshooting, tuning, high availability.
- **IT operations** — beyond the database: networking, Kubernetes, systems underneath.
- **Cove's digital twin** — building my AI secretary. Turning behavior into code with Vibe Coding; a personal agent.
- **IT/IC industry outlook** — beyond code: compute trends and how hardware reshapes software and the industry.
- **Life & growth** — the garden after the job title comes off. Cognitive shifts from working and living at once.

## Labels on technical titles

To lower the cost of choosing what to read, technical posts carry clear tags in the title:

- **[Cheat sheet]** — short command manuals. Grab and go when something breaks.
- **[Deep dive]** — long essays on underlying logic. Not just translated docs: corrections and real pitfalls, for immersive reading.
- **[Members]** — a quiet paid loop (about the price of a coffee). Curated maps and quarterly internals.
- **[Challenge]** — the stage belongs to the comments. Hard questions pinned for everyone to crack.

Here, technique is not only a way to earn a living; it is systems thinking for understanding the world. Whether a tangled outage or a small riddle in ordinary life, I will keep recording and exploring in this modest plot.

Hello, I'm Cove. I can cover it.
